5. KEY TECHNICAL LESSONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Differentiating between active memory states (`list-units`) and disk configuration states (`list-unit-files`) is essential for mapping persistent attack vectors.
* Default Linux distributions enable cloud-provisioning tools (`cloud-init`) that are redundant in desktop/WSL environments and should be trimmed.
* Disabling unwanted daemons cuts memory usage, speeds up system initialization, and reduces overall exposure to zero-day service exploits.
