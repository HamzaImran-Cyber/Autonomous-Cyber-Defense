5. WHAT I LEARNED TODAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* `tshark -z conv,tcp` aggregates raw frames into bidirectional host-to-host flows, 
  allowing rapid detection of asymmetric traffic ratios.
* A high volume of SYN packets (`syn==1 && ack==0`) lacking corresponding ACK or 
  payload responses is the primary signature of port scanning and SYN flooding.
* `ss -s` provides real-time kernel memory socket summaries, enabling SOC analysts 
  to verify if a host socket queue is under resource exhaustion.
