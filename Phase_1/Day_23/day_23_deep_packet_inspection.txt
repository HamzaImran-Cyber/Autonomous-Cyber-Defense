5. WHAT I LEARNED TODAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) reveals cleartext Application-Layer (L7) payloads 
  that standard Layer 3/4 header analysis misses.
* `tshark` enables programmatic, CLI-driven Wireshark dissection, allowing automated 
  SOC telemetry parsing without a Graphical User Interface (GUI).
* Display filters (`-Y`) operate post-capture on dissected fields, whereas capture 
  filters (`-f` / BPF) operate inside kernel space prior to byte processing.
