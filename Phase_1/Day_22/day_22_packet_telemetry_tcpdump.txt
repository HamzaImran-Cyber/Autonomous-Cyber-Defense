    before the virtual NIC driver calculates the final checksum value.

5. WHAT I LEARNED TODAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Berkeley Packet Filters (BPF) operate directly in Linux kernel space, allowing 
  high-performance packet filtering before saving to disk.
* Binary PCAP files preserve full Layer 3 and Layer 4 header payloads for offline 
  forensic analysis.
* TCP SYN packets (`Flags [S]`) mark connection setup attempts and serve as primary 
  signatures for port scans and network probes.
